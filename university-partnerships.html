The model

How partnerships work.

Lightweight to set up, run with your existing career services or student societies, and shaped around your calendar.

01

Scope a program together

A short call with your careers team or student society to pick a format — live interview nights, workshop series, or recorded sessions for your platforms.

02

We bring the guests

We match speakers from our interview network to the industries and roles your students actually want, and handle outreach and prep.

03

Run it on campus

Events run live at your university or online, co-branded with your institution. Students get direct Q&A time, not just a talk.

04

Keep the content

Recordings and written recaps stay available to your students afterwards, so each event compounds into a growing career resource.
