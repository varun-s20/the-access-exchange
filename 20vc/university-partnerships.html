How it works

Four steps, scoped around your semester rather than ours. Nothing here needs a procurement cycle to start.

01

We talk

A short call to understand your programme, your students, and what success looks like for your institution.

02

We shape a partnership

Interview content, guest access, or a joint session — built around what your students actually need.

03

We launch together

Co-branded content, a campus session, or curated interview access, rolled out on a timeline that works for your semester.

04

We keep it going

New interviews, ongoing access, and a relationship that grows past a single event.

What a partnership can include

Pick the parts that fit. Most partnerships start with one and add the others once a cohort has been through it.

Guest sessions

A practitioner from the series, on campus or online, matched to your course mix. Talk, then a long Q&A — the part students remember.

Cohort workshops

Applications, portfolios and interview practice, run against how hiring actually works this year rather than a generic template.

A content library you keep

Curated interviews, recordings from your own sessions and the accompanying notes — yours to reuse with next year’s cohort.

Co-branded interviews

We record with your alumni and your industry contacts, and the episodes carry your institution alongside ours.

Panels and careers weeks

A slot in an existing careers week, or a full panel built around the routes your students keep asking about.

Society collaborations

Smaller, student-led formats for computing, data and design societies, where the room sets the agenda.

Who we work with

What your team walks away with

The point of a partnership is not the event. It is what is still usable in your careers office once the room has emptied.
