Bring the industry onto campus.

We work with careers services, departments and student societies to turn the interview series into something students can actually take part in — live sessions, guided workshops and a library your institution keeps.

  • 01Scope a programme together — term dates, cohort size, the gaps you already know about.
  • 02We bring the guests — practitioners from the series, matched to your course mix.
  • 03Run it on campus or online — talks, Q&A, portfolio and interview workshops.
