What The Access Exchange actually is

A tech-focused media and professional-development platform, running through two vehicles: an interview series, and partnerships with universities.

1

The interview series

Long-form conversations with people working in tech. What the job involves, how they got in, what they would do differently.

2

University partnerships

Programmes built with careers services and departments — guest sessions, workshops, and a content library the institution keeps.

3

Specific, not motivational

Real numbers, real timelines, real rejections. Advice you can act on this week rather than agree with and forget.
