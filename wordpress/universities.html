01 - Why it matters

Some lessons only experience can teach.

The classroom builds knowledge. The Access Exchange brings students closer to the people who have lived the decisions, changes and challenges behind it.

What a leader knows about a turning point in their career, an industry shift they had to read correctly, or a call they got wrong and had to recover from - none of that is in a syllabus. It is held by the people who were there, and it usually reaches students only if they happen to know somebody.

Bring the experience of industry closer to the people preparing to shape it.

Everything below is the mechanics of getting the right person in front of the right audience, on your timetable.

02 - The format

One live conversation, on your campus.

The Live Campus Conversation is how most institutions work with us. An experienced professional comes to your campus for a live interview in front of your students. The floor then opens for student questions, and the session is professionally filmed for The Access Exchange.

  • 01
    A live conversation on campusAn in-depth interview with an experienced professional, in a room your students already use - the decisions, turning points and lessons behind the career rather than the headline version of it.
  • 02
    Student Q&ADirect questions from your students. The Q&A is the part they remember, so we leave real time for it.
  • 03
    Professionally filmedThe session is professionally filmed for The Access Exchange, so the conversation can reach people well beyond the room.

Additional options. Where a different shape suits your students better, these are available alongside the Live Campus Conversation or instead of it.

Talks and discussions

  • Leadership Talk + Q&A - a focused leadership session, followed by direct student questions.
  • Moderated Leadership Discussion - a deeper, moderated discussion around experience, perspective and the thinking behind the work.

Built around your program

  • Industry Session - a topic-driven program with experienced practitioners, built around a theme your students or your department are already working on.
  • Custom Campus Experience - programming shaped around your students, academic programs, industry themes or institutional objectives.

03 - How it comes together

Four steps, none of which need a procurement cycle.

Most engagements start with one person inside an institution who already knows which conversation their students are missing. Usually it begins with an email, not a tender.

  • 01
    We talkA short call to understand your program, your audience, and what success looks like for your institution. No deck.
  • 02
    We shape it togetherThe format, the theme and the kind of speaker, built around what your students actually need this year.
  • 03
    We run itLive on your campus, in front of your students, on a timeline that fits your term and your assessment periods.
  • 04
    We share itSessions may be professionally filmed and published by The Access Exchange, with appropriate permissions. A named contact stays with the relationship throughout.

05 - Questions we get first

The questions institutions ask first.

How much lead time do you need?

About two weeks for a single leadership talk. Four to six for a custom campus experience, mostly because matching the right person takes longer than booking a room.

Can you work around our term dates?

Yes. Tell us your assessment periods and reading weeks on the first call and we plan around them.

What if our audience is small?

Twenty engaged students in a seminar room is a better session than three hundred in a lecture theater. Audience size is not the filter and there is no minimum.

Is the session filmed?

Yes. Sessions may be professionally filmed and published by The Access Exchange, with appropriate permissions.

Tell us about your institution.

Two or three lines is enough to start. If it is not a fit we will say so quickly, rather than book a call to say it slowly.
