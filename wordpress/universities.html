01 - Why it matters

Some lessons only experience can teach.

The classroom builds knowledge. The Access Exchange brings students closer to the people who have lived the decisions, changes and challenges behind it.

What a leader knows about a turning point in their career, an industry shift they had to read correctly, or a call they got wrong and had to recover from - none of that is in a syllabus. It is held by the people who were there, and it usually reaches students only if they happen to know somebody.

Bring the experience of industry closer to the people preparing to shape it.

Everything below is the mechanics of getting the right person in front of the right audience, on your timetable.

02 - The formats

Four ways to bring it into the room.

Most institutions start with one of these and add others once an audience has been through it. Nothing here is a bundle you have to take whole.

  • 01
    Leadership Talk + Q&AA focused leadership session, followed by direct student questions. The Q&A is the part they remember, so we leave real time for it.
  • 02
    Moderated Leadership DiscussionA deeper, moderated discussion around experience, perspective and the thinking behind the work rather than the headline version of it.
  • 03
    Industry SessionA topic-driven programme with experienced practitioners, built around a theme your students or your department are already working on.
  • 04
    Custom Campus ExperienceProgramming shaped around your students, academic programmes, industry themes or institutional objectives. If none of the above is the right shape, this is.

03 - How it comes together

Four steps, none of which need a procurement cycle.

Most engagements start with one person inside an institution who already knows which conversation their students are missing. Usually it begins with an email, not a tender.

  • 01
    We talkA short call to understand your programme, your audience, and what success looks like for your institution. No deck.
  • 02
    We shape it togetherThe format, the theme and the kind of speaker, built around what your students actually need this year.
  • 03
    We run itOn campus or online, on a timeline that fits your term and your assessment periods.
  • 04
    You keep itRecording and materials where the speaker agrees, and a named contact who stays with the relationship.

Academic and institutional

Student-facing

05 - Questions we get first

The questions institutions ask first.

What does an engagement cost?

It depends on the format and the number of sessions. The first conversation is free, and we will give you the number on that call rather than after three meetings and a proposal document.

Do students pay anything?

Never. Institutions arrange the programming; students are never charged to attend or to watch.

How much lead time do you need?

About two weeks for a single leadership talk. Four to six for a custom campus experience, mostly because matching the right person takes longer than booking a room.

Can you work around our term dates?

Yes. Tell us your assessment periods and reading weeks on the first call and we plan around them.

What if our audience is small?

Twenty engaged students in a seminar room is a better session than three hundred in a lecture theatre. Audience size is not the filter and there is no minimum.

Can we record the session?

Usually yes, with the speaker's agreement. Anything recorded with your students is yours to reuse.

Tell us about your institution.

Two or three lines is enough to start. If it is not a fit we will say so quickly, rather than book a call to say it slowly.
