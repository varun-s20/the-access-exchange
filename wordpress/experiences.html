What we put in the room

Four ways people end up in the same room.

01

Leadership discussions

An accomplished leader, in depth, in front of people who came to listen properly. Closer to the Interview Series than to a keynote.

02

Industry panels

Several practitioners who genuinely disagree, moderated so the disagreement is the useful part rather than the awkward part.

03

Learning experiences

Smaller and more demanding. A working session where people leave with something they can apply, not just something they enjoyed.

04

Access Exchange events

Our own gatherings, built so the conversations that start on stage carry on in the room afterwards. Proximity is the whole product.

A recorded conversation travels further. A live one changes something on the day.

People ask the question they would not type. They meet the person they would not have emailed. They hear a leader think out loud rather than deliver a position.

Access is easiest to create when everyone is already in the same room.
