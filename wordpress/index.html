06How we work

What we hold every interview to.

01

Specific over inspiring

Every interview is pushed toward detail: the actual timeline, the actual rejection, the actual conversation that changed something. If a story only works as encouragement, it does not make the edit.

02

Mid-career, not just the summit

Founders and executives are well covered elsewhere. Most of our guests are two to ten years in, close enough to the start that they still remember what was confusing.

03

Guests choose what stays in

People are honest when honesty is safe. Every guest reviews their cut before it goes out, and anything they want removed is removed, no negotiation.

04

Free at the point of use

The interviews stay open. Institutions pay for the programmes we run with them, not for their students to watch a conversation.

07University partnerships
