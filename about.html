About us

Access shouldn't depend on who you know.

The Access Exchange exists because the most useful career knowledge in tech still travels through private networks — and too many talented people are outside them.

Our story

Why we started this.

Ask anyone how they got their break in tech and you'll usually hear the same thing: someone opened a door. A referral, an honest coffee chat, a mentor who explained what the job description didn't say. That kind of access is career-changing — and wildly unevenly distributed.

The advice itself isn't secret. Hiring managers are happy to explain what they look for. Founders will tell you exactly how they got their first customers. But those conversations happen in places most students and career-switchers never get invited to.

So we started recording them. The Access Exchange turns the conversations that usually happen behind closed doors into a public interview series, distills them into practical written insights, and brings the people behind them onto university campuses — so the next generation of builders gets the same signal, regardless of their starting network.

Why we exist

Talent is everywhere. Access isn't. We're building the exchange where the two meet: real practitioners sharing what actually works, and emerging professionals who can put it to use immediately.
