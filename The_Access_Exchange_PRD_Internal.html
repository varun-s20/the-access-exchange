§ 03 · Background & problem
Why this exists.
A startup platform needs a credible front door before its first interviews and university partnerships go live.
The Access Exchange is launching two things at once — an interview series and a set of university partnerships — and needs a site that explains both clearly to two very different audiences, with a simple way for either to get in touch. The build itself pivoted mid-conversation: from a stock e-commerce gig template to a fully bespoke, budget-conscious custom build.
Mid-engagement pivot: the seller's six-page gig defaulted to e-commerce and a 30-product catalog. The client rejected both in the very first message and asked for inquiry forms, SEO, and video integration instead.
